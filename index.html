Tribute to Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam, known as the *Missile Man of India*, was one of the most loved presidents of India. He inspired millions with his simplicity, dedication, and powerful vision for the youth.

Why I Admire Him

  • He believed in the power of dreams.
  • He motivated the youth to work hard.
  • He lived a simple and humble life.

"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."
